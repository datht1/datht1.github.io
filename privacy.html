Privacy policy – Hoithoai Uploader

Last updated: 20 October 2026

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl to upload and edit videos, thumbnails, captions, playlists and channel settings of the channel “Hội Thoại Tiếng Trung Mỗi Ngày”. It reads channel and video statistics to report on the channel.

Where data is kept

The OAuth token and the list of uploaded videos are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app uses Google user data only to provide the functions above, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. The app is not offered to the public.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or Google security settings. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer removes the stored credentials.
